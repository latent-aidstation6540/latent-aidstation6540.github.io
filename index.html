🔍 What Is SoDam_O-Brain-Codex?

SoDam_O-Brain-Codex is your personal memory companion for Codex, an AI assistant. It acts like a smart notebook that automatically remembers everything you and Codex talk about. Every question, every answer, every preference you express — all stored safely on your computer.

Think of it as a searchable brain for your AI conversations. You can instantly find that solution Codex gave you three weeks ago, or see how your ideas connect through beautiful visual maps.

✨ Key Features

💾 Local-First Storage

Your data stays on your PC. No cloud, no internet required. Complete privacy.

🔎 Semantic Search

Search conversations using natural language, not just keywords. Find meaning, not just matches.

📊 2D/3D Knowledge Graphs

See how topics, ideas, and conversations connect through interactive visual maps.

📅 Timeline View

Scroll through your conversation history chronologically to see your AI journey.

🔄 Automatic Backup

Your memories are automatically backed up, so you never lose important context.

⚡ Smart Preferences

Codex learns your likes and dislikes from stored conversations, giving better responses.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your data is 100% local. SoDam_O-Brain-Codex stores everything in a SQLite database on your computer. There is no cloud synchronization, no data collection, and no internet connection required for the core functionality.
